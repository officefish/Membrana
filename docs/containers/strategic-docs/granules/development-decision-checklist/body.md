## Decision Checklist

1. Can one executor finish and verify it in one coherent review atom?
   Use `one shot`.
2. Does the value include accountable role-specific review, owner ratification
   or procedure evidence?
   Use `membrana-local-sprint`.
3. Is the work a chain of sprints with a special topology?
   Choose the matching container and write its brief before execution.
4. Is it after-hours bounded hardening?
   Use `night build`.
